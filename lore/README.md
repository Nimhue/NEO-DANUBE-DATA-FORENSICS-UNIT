# DFU case briefings

```
> HALCYON DYNAMICS · INCIDENT HAL-2077
> CLASSIFICATION: INTERNAL · DFU EYES ONLY
```

## Situation

At 03:12 on 15 March 2077, Halcyon's audit daemon reported that ledger balances no longer reconciled
with settlement totals. The ledger history shows nothing unusual: every commit for the past fourteen
days is labelled *routine reconciliation* or *night shift settlement*.

Two days later, a courier was found dead in the Csepel Docks. Her cyberdeck held a damaged data
shard containing a copy of the ledger as it stood on 1 March, *before* the suspicious window.
Someone was keeping insurance.

## Standing orders

1. **Preserve before you analyse.** Raw evidence stays in `evidence_raw`, untouched.
2. **Record every repair.** A recovered value without a recorded rule is fabrication.
3. **Two witnesses.** Don't accuse on a single weak signal.
4. **Undo surgically.** Honest work done during the incident must survive the fix.
5. **Need to know.** Rookies don't see PII or the prime suspect's name.
